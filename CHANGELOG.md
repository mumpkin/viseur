## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Crosshair overlay visible above fullscreen games, borderless games, Proton/XWayland,
  fullscreen video and F11 applications, with direct scanout disabled while the crosshair is shown
- Cross, dot and circle shapes with color, opacity, outline and fine offset
- Profiles with a live preview, 5 editable presets and a default profile at login
- Keyboard shortcuts to show or hide the crosshair, switch to the next or previous profile,
  select profiles #1 to #4 and hold a temporary profile
- Right-click temporary profile with an Off, Hold or Toggle mode, from the panel menu or the preferences
- Target monitor selection: primary, monitor of the focused window, or a given number
- `viseurctl` command-line helper, install and uninstall scripts and an Arch Linux PKGBUILD
- Support for GNOME Shell 50 and 51
